# Third-party data notices

## GADM administrative boundaries

Barangay geometry is extracted from the GADM 3.6 Philippines third-level administrative layer and corresponds to the 2018 GADM reference map for Manito. GADM permits academic and other non-commercial use. Redistribution or commercial use is not allowed without prior permission.

Sources: <https://gadm.org/maps/PHL/albay/manito_3.html> and <https://gadm.org/license.html>

## Statistical and demographic data

Barangay population, classification, and geographic codes are from the Philippine Statistics Authority PSGC and 2024 POPCEN. Historical population, sex ratio, and dependency ratio are from PSA census publications. PSA site content is identified by PSA as CC BY 4.0 unless otherwise stated.

Sources: <https://psa.gov.ph/classification/psgc/barangays/0500511000>, <https://rsso05.psa.gov.ph/content/men-and-women-statistics-albay-2026>, and <https://rsso05.psa.gov.ph/system/files/publication/2025RSET5.pdf>

## Economic context

Manito's municipal income class is taken from the current PSA PSGC listing. The locally sourced revenue series covers 2017–2019 and is from a BLGF SGLG annex. The Bacon–Manito geothermal project status is from the Department of Energy's December 2024 awarded geothermal project list. Reference years are shown with the values in the interface.

Sources: <https://psa.gov.ph/classification/psgc/citimuni/0500500000>, <https://blgf.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/2021-SGLG-Average-Growth-on-LSR_Municipality.pdf>, and <https://legacy.doe.gov.ph/sites/default/files/pdf/renewable_energy/Awarded%20Geothermal%20as%20of%20Dec.%202024.pdf>
